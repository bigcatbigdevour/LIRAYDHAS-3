LIRAYDHAS — REVIEW NOTES

What the app does
-----------------
Liraydhas is a personal-use observational tool that combines real
astrology (natal chart + daily transits computed locally from
ephemeris data), Human Design (body graph + gates + channels), and
seven life cycles (Saturn, Jupiter, Chiron, etc.) into one daily
written reading and a series of chart visualisations.

The entire computation runs on-device. The only thing sent to our
server is the user's birth data (date, time, lat/lon), and only when
they request a written reading; we do not retain it after generating
the response.

How to test
-----------
1. Open the app. The onboarding flow asks for birth date, time
   (optional), and birth place. Use any real birth data — the app
   computes the chart immediately.
2. Tap through to /today. A daily reading streams in within a few
   seconds.
3. /chart, /year, /polarity, /arcs all render from the same blueprint
   without further input.

Sample test account
-------------------
No account creation is needed. The app has no login. All data lives
locally on the device. To test Pro features without a sandbox Apple
ID purchase, tap "start free trial" on /pro — this grants 7 days of
Pro access locally without going through StoreKit.

Pro features (in-app subscription)
----------------------------------
Pro unlocks:
  · /year — the full year-ahead reading
  · /compat — synastry readings between two saved partners
  · /chart — the "your chart's standalone reading" paragraph
  · Pull-to-refresh on daily readings

Free tier includes the daily reading, chart visualisations, polarity
stack, arcs diagram, journal, and saved entries.

Subscriptions
-------------
  · Monthly: $4.99 / month, auto-renewing
  · Annual: $39.99 / year, auto-renewing

Auto-renew terms are linked from the paywall (see /pro). Cancellation
is handled through the user's Apple ID subscriptions page, per Apple
guidelines.

Privacy
-------
  · Birth data: sent to the server ONLY when generating a written
    paragraph. Not retained — endpoints are stateless.
  · Journal entries, photos, voice notes: stored locally on the
    device (IndexedDB / localStorage). Never sent to the server.
  · Push subscription endpoints: stored in Vercel KV so we can
    deliver the optional daily reading reminder. Not linked to any
    other identifier.
  · No tracking, no advertising SDKs, no analytics — see the
    bundled PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy.

Account deletion
----------------
/about → "delete all my data" wipes blueprint, every saved reading,
all photos / voice notes from IndexedDB, every UI flag, and
unregisters push notifications.

What this app is NOT
--------------------
Not a mental-health service, not a medical service, not a substitute
for professional support. The disclaimer is visible on /support
and surfaces during onboarding. The voice of the readings is
"quiet observer" — declarative observations, never advice, never
predictions, never crisis intervention.

Support
-------
support@liraydhas.app  (see /support inside the app)

Encryption
----------
The app uses HTTPS only — no proprietary cryptography. The bundled
Info.plist sets ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false so the export
compliance exemption applies.

Third-party content
-------------------
The astrology and Human Design systems referenced are public-domain
traditional knowledge. The text of readings is generated per-request
by Anthropic's Claude model from the user's chart numbers; no
external text is reproduced.
