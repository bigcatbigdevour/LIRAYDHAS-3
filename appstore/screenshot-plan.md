# Screenshot plan

App Store Connect requires screenshots for at least one device size in the iPhone family. Provide the 6.7" (iPhone 15/16 Pro Max, 1320×2868 portrait) set; Apple auto-resizes to the smaller sizes if you skip them.

The shots below are the strongest five. Capture in the iOS Simulator at 1320×2868 with the status bar showing 9:41, full battery, full signal (Simulator → Device → Erase All Content & Settings → relaunch will reset to defaults; Simulator → I/O → Status Bar overrides those).

1. **/today** — Header showing date + moon phase + retrogrades, the "today is..." italic, the chapter banner, and the LLM paragraph fully visible. If possible, scroll so the "tonight's question" peeks at the bottom.

2. **/chart** — The full bodygraph centered in frame with the legend below. Open with a gate tapped so the explanation card is visible.

3. **/arcs** — The lifetime arcs diagram with the today-pin lit and a station diamond selected (use age 29 for the Saturn return diamond — recognizable).

4. **/polarity** — The full polarity bars with most bars in mid-state (so the rising/descending mix is visible) plus the headline mood line at the top.

5. **/saved** — A populated journal with 3–5 entries, at least one pinned with the wine accent, the heatmap visible, and at least one entry with a photo thumbnail.

Each screenshot can include a short caption overlay in the App Store Connect editor:
  1. "One paragraph a day, from your own chart."
  2. "Your body chart, tappable."
  3. "Every cycle of a lifetime, drawn."
  4. "The tides under your year."
  5. "A real private journal — yours."

Lowercase, no exclamation points, matches the brand voice.
